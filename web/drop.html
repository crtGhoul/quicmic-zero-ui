Nearby & ready

How does tap-to-connect work?
  1. Open this page on both devices (same Wi-Fi works best).
  2. Each device shows up in the other's Nearby & ready list — tap one to call it.
  3. Accept on the other device. Done — send anything.

A free matchmaker server just introduces your devices — your photos and files then fly directly between them (WebRTC), never through the server. Devices on your network are marked nearby. Want to stay invisible? Turn on 🙈 Private mode above — you can still call out. First visit of the day can take ~30–60s: the free server sleeps when idle. No devices showing? You can always pair with a Share / Receive code instead.
