❓ The 101 Guide

Everything you need to go from "what is this" to talking into your phone and hearing it on your PC. Short sections, no jargon.

What QuicMic is

QuicMic turns your phone into a wireless microphone for your PC. You talk into the phone, and your PC hears it as a microphone input — for Discord, games, recording, whatever you like.

Everything stays on your own home WiFi. No cloud, no account, no internet needed once it's set up.

First run on the PC

  1. Run the QuicMic exe on your PC. A console window opens showing a big QR code and a 6-digit PIN.
  2. When Windows Firewall asks, allow it on private networks. Skipping this is the most common reason phones can't connect.
  3. Leave that window open — it's the server. The QR code and PIN live there.

Pairing your phone

  1. Install the QuicMic Android app and open it.
  2. Tap Scan QR code and point it at the code in the PC window. It fills in the address and PIN for you.
  3. No camera handy? Open the /qr page on any device to see the code bigger, or type the PC's address and PIN in by hand.
  4. Tap connect. That's it — you're paired.

The golden rule: same WiFi

Both devices must be on the same WiFi network. If your phone is on mobile data, or on a guest network while the PC is on the main one, they can't see each other and pairing will just time out.

Getting your voice into Discord and other apps

Your phone's audio arrives at the PC, but apps like Discord need somewhere to pick it up from. That's what a virtual audio cable is for:

  1. Install a virtual audio cable on the PC (VB-Cable is a popular free one).
  2. Point QuicMic's output at it (the Speaker screen has a device picker and a test tone so you can check it's alive).
  3. In Discord (or Windows sound settings), set your input device to that virtual cable. Now your phone is your mic.

The QR shows a weird address (like 10.2.0.2)?

Sometimes the PC picks the wrong network address for the QR code — VPNs and virtual adapters confuse it. If the address doesn't look like your home network (usually 192.168.something), fix it like this:

  1. Close the QuicMic window.
  2. On the PC, open a command prompt and run ipconfig to find your real address (the 192.168.something one).
  3. Start the exe again with that address forced:
    quicmic.exe --ip 192.168.1.50 (use your real address, not this example)
  4. Scan the fresh QR code. It'll show the right address now.

Quick troubleshooting

"Could not verify server" or it just times out
Your phone can't reach the PC. Check: same WiFi? PC app actually running? Firewall allowed? Right address?
It paired before but won't now
The PIN probably rotated. Grab the fresh one from the PC window and try again.
Choppy or delayed audio
Usually WiFi congestion. The Mic screen's latency-recovery slider is made for exactly this — nudge it up.
No sound in Discord but the app looks connected
Discord is probably listening to the wrong input. Check its voice settings and point it at the virtual cable.

Still stuck? The Mic screen has a Diagnose connection button that checks the common problems for you.
