GUIDE

Get talking in minutes.

1. Install the virtual mic PC side

QuicMic plays your phone's audio into a virtual microphone on the PC, and Discord listens on its other end. On Windows that is VB-Audio Virtual Cable ("CABLE Input" → "CABLE Output"). It is free.

vb-audio.com/Cable — reboot if the installer asks, then restart QuicMic.

2. Pair this phone

On the PC, open the Pair tab and scan the QR code with your phone's camera. Accept the certificate warning — it is your own PC, so the warning is expected. Then tap the mic button to start talking.

3. Pick the mic in Discord

Discord → Settings → Voice & Video → Input Device → your phone's name. If you renamed the mic and Discord still shows the old name, restart Discord — it caches device names.

Windows Firewall

The first time QuicMic runs, Windows asks to allow it: tick Private networks. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.

Clicked Block by accident, or never saw the prompt? Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Windows Security → Firewall → "Allow an app through firewall" → find QuicMic → check Private. Then restart QuicMic.

Troubleshooting

Paired but Discord hears nothing. 1) PC Status tab: Connected, packet counts rising? 2) Devices tab: mic output "CABLE Input"? 3) Discord input = your phone's name (not Default)? 4) Just renamed? Restart Discord.

Choppy or robotic audio. Wi-Fi congestion — move closer to the router or use 5 GHz. The PC Diagnostics tab shows packet-loss %.

Certificate warning keeps coming back. Normal: the certificate renews every 14 days and when your network changes. Accept it again.

"Another client is already connected." One phone at a time — close the mic page on the other device.
